# Office: living windows (round after vbnh)

## Objective
The owner reviewed vbnh's three directions (atelier/vbnh: D-A Lamplight, D-B Lit shrines, D-C Night street) and set the direction (ruling R-17, overrule #8), in their words: "i like the idea of the windows as our icon being included with the glow to indicate working. the lamp and silhouettes make them look lifeless and sad drowning in work. and window icons are okay but no personalisation. just objects."

So: every agent is a window in the shape of our N1 mark, glowing to show it is working (keep D-B's glow, which fills with the task's stage). Your job is to give those windows personality, so each one feels alive and enjoyable, while staying anonymous (R-15: no names, faces or fixed identities to get attached to).

## What to make
Three clearly different ways to personalise the windows, all on the same D-B base, for the owner to pick one. For example (find your own, or better):
- P1 What lives in the window: small objects and life on the sill and behind the glass (a plant, a mug, books, a curtain) that differ by role and change with state.
- P2 Character in the light: each window's glow has its own temperament (warmth, flicker, a blind half drawn), and moments of life (a curtain twitch when it finishes, a light switching off when it goes off duty).
- P3 A cheerful, anonymous presence: a small figure or creature glimpsed moving inside, only ever upbeat and busy, never slumped.
Nothing may look burdened, gloomy or sad: no slumped silhouettes, no heads in hands. The mood is a lively, warm building at night, calm overall (charter, AC-7).

For each variant: the real Office at 390 (first) and 1440, dark, with every state: working, quiet a long while, needs you, waiting, paused, off, finished. Plus a short motion demo (webm and contact sheet) and reduced motion respected. Cards follow F-3: no "Try N of M", show the stage and latest step in words.

## Hand-off and done means
- One pick page atelier/design/office-windows/index.html, ids P1-P3, your top pick in three sentences.
- At most 8 PNGs in evidence/, phone width (390), readable on a phone; they go straight to the owner's Inbox (P1, P2, P3 Office, then the every-state sheets, then one motion strip).
- A DESIGN.md › Office windows section, with two critique rounds (R-1) and an accessibility pass.
- `atelier verify design` passes; headless browsers only, never open windows on the owner's screen.
- Criteria AC-7, AC-13, AC-17.

## Out of scope
dashboard/ and lib/ (building comes after the owner picks); other screens; the lamp and night-street directions.
