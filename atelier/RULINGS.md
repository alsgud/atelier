# Rulings

The owner's decisions, numbered. Every agent follows them. Where a ruling can be checked mechanically, it also exists as a criterion.

## R-1: Direction B (one at a time: the top decision shown large, a decision dock, the rest of the queue in a quiet side column) is the chosen feel. All design work must be production grade, holding up next to Linear and Vercel: references studied first (Mobbin and the brief's taste references), a written visual system (tokens, type and spacing scales, components in every state) before screens, real content and real images instead of placeholder boxes, and at least two critique rounds using the design-critique and accessibility-review skills before anything reaches the owner. The lead's briefs never skip these steps to save money.

## R-3: Feel and motion are first-class design work, not polish. Every design hand-off includes working demos of the key interactions (opening an item, deciding, the next item arriving, undo, inbox zero, live agent progress, switching screens) as recordings the owner can watch, offered in a few clearly different, refined variants when the feel is not yet settled. Motion is quick and purposeful, never decorative, and respects reduced motion.
_2026-10-24 · owner, chat 2026-10-25: 'Feeling and animations are still quite lacking... show me a few more demos and much more refined versions.'_
