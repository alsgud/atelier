Pick Atelier’s logo, colours and layout

Everything here is the Flow Inbox you chose, with its motion unchanged. Each option has a number. Pick one of each, or a whole combination.

Reply with, for example, L7 + C2 + Y1 or Combo B. Mixing is fine: Combo A, but L12.

Logos

Four ideas, four ways to draw each. Every mark is shown at 128, 32 and 16 px (the browser tab), with its wordmark on light and dark, and in the real top bar of the Inbox at 1440 and 390.

Colour schemes

Each scheme is a neutral ramp, one brand accent, and the four meanings. Waiting is neutral on purpose; only working, done and needs-you carry colour (R4). The sample is the item header and dock from the Inbox.

Contrast

Measured from the colours on this page as it loads, not typed in. Text needs 4.5:1 (WCAG AA); rings, borders and progress fills need 3:1.

Layouts

Three ways to arrange the same Inbox. Only the frame moves: the item, the decision dock, the queue and Flow’s motion are identical in all three. Shown in L1 and C1 so only the layout differs.

Recommended combinations

Four whole looks, each a logo, a scheme and a layout that belong together. Each is the full Inbox in light and dark, and a recorded demo of the same journey (open an item, decide it, undo, check the Office, clear the inbox) so you see colour and Flow’s motion together.
