Logos

Four ideas, four ways to draw each. Every mark is shown at 128, 32 and 16 px (the browser tab), with its wordmark on light and dark, and in the real top bar of the Inbox at 1440 and 390.

Colour schemes

Each scheme is a neutral ramp, one brand accent, and the four meanings. Waiting is neutral on purpose; only working, done and needs-you carry colour (R4). The sample is the item header and dock from the Inbox.

Contrast

Measured from the colours on this page as it loads, not typed in. Text needs 4.5:1 (WCAG AA); rings, borders and progress fills need 3:1.
