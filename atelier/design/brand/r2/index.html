What I looked at first

Public pages only. What each does well, and what to stay away from. Nothing here was copied.

Marks

Eleven marks. Each has its one-line idea, sizes from 128 to 16 px (the browser tab) in dark and light, the lockup, and a crop of the real Inbox sidebar in dark. Where a mark gets busy small, it has its own small cut, used at 20 px and under.

Sketched and set aside

Drawn, looked at small and large, and dropped before refining, so you can see what else was tried.

    Colour schemes

    Dark is the main mode and the default; each scheme also has a light mode that stays off white. The brand accent is only on the mark and the focus ring. On content, colour still means only working (lilac), done (green) and needs you (red); waiting is neutral.

    Contrast

    Measured from the colours on this page as it loads, not typed in. Text needs 4.5:1 (WCAG AA); rings, borders and progress fills need 3:1.

    Combinations

    Three whole looks, each a mark and a scheme, all in the Y1 sidebar layout you chose. Each is the full Inbox in dark at 1440 and 390, and light at 390, so you can see whether light suits the phone better. Each has a recorded demo of the same journey (open an item, decide, undo, check the Office, clear the inbox) with its key frames.
