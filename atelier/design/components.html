Components

Everything the Inbox is built from, in every state. Values come from tokens.css; classes from components.css. Colour means something: lilac is working, red needs you or went wrong, green is done. Everything else is a warm neutral.

Colour roles

Roles, not hex: a screen asks for “text-2” or “needs”, never a colour. All text pairs measured at 4.5:1 or better in both themes (table in DESIGN.md).

Navigation

Desktop: a sidebar (≥ 1280 px). Tablet: a top bar with underline tabs. Phone: a bottom tab bar. The Inbox count turns red only when something needs you now; nothing else in the chrome is coloured except a working project's lilac dot.

Queue item

One plain sentence, the project and the age. A kind glyph lets you tell a design from a question at a glance; a red dot only on what needs you now. Selection is neutral, because lilac means working.

Working now

Lilac, live, and a progress figure that means something (“1,240 of 1,800 clips”, not “iteration 2 of 6”).

default · hover · quiet (no progress figure)

Preview frame

The real thing, never a drawn stand-in. Opens the full page from the view port in a new tab. States: default, hover, focus, recording, loading, couldn't load.

Decision dock

Where every decision happens. Sticks to the bottom of the item on desktop and above the tab bar on the phone, so the buttons are always in reach.

Toasts and banner
